# Estimated tax

Who pays quarterly, and how the safe harbor avoids an underpayment penalty. Percentages and
dates are year-specific — verify.

## Who pays

Taxpayers whose liability is not sufficiently covered by withholding — the self-employed,
many businesses, investors — generally must pay **quarterly estimated tax** (individuals use
**Form 1040-ES**). Installments follow a quarterly schedule keyed to the tax year.

## Safe harbor

An individual generally avoids the **underpayment penalty** by paying — through withholding
plus estimates — at least the **safe-harbor amount**: broadly a set percentage of the
**current-year** liability, **or** of the **prior-year** liability (a higher prior-year
percentage applies above an AGI threshold). The exact percentages and threshold are
year-specific — verify.

## Withholding is treated as even

Withholding is generally treated as paid **evenly across the year**, so increasing
withholding late in the year can cure an earlier shortfall in a way a late estimate cannot.

## IRS source

Verify against **IRC §6654** (individuals) and **§6655** (corporations), the **Form 1040-ES**
and **Form 2210** instructions, and **Pub 505 (Tax Withholding and Estimated Tax)**.
