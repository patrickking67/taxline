# Depreciation

How the cost of business property is recovered over time. Class lives, dollar limits, and
bonus percentages are year-specific — verify against the IRS source.

## MACRS (§168)

Most tangible business property is depreciated under the **Modified Accelerated Cost
Recovery System (MACRS)** over a **class life** — for example 5-year, 7-year, 27.5-year
(residential rental), or 39-year (nonresidential real property) — using a prescribed method
and convention. **Land is not depreciable.**

## §179 expensing

**IRC §179** lets a taxpayer **elect to expense** the cost of qualifying property in the year
it is placed in service, up to an **annual dollar limit**, with a **phase-out** above an
investment threshold and a cap at business income. The exact limit and threshold change
yearly — verify.

## Bonus depreciation (§168(k))

**Bonus depreciation** allows an additional first-year deduction on qualifying property at a
statutory percentage that is **scheduled to change year to year**. Confirm the current-year
percentage and which property qualifies.

## Basis and recapture

Depreciation is computed on the property's **basis** (generally cost, adjusted). Track basis
and accumulated depreciation per asset; a later disposition can trigger **depreciation
recapture** taxed as ordinary income.

## IRS source

Verify against **IRC §§167, 168, 179**, **Pub 946 (How to Depreciate Property)**, and the
**Form 4562** instructions for current limits, conventions, and bonus percentages.
